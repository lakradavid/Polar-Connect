About Polar Science

Why Polar Regions Matter to All of Us

Earth's polar regions — the Arctic in the north and Antarctica in the south — are not remote, irrelevant wilderness. They are the planet's climate regulators, housing 90% of its fresh water and influencing global weather patterns, sea levels and biodiversity.

India has maintained an active presence in Antarctic research since 1981, operating Maitri and Bharati stations and contributing decades of scientific data to the global understanding of polar dynamics, climate change and oceanography.
